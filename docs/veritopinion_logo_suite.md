# Veritopinion – Brand & Logo Identity Suite

This documentation outlines the production-ready brand assets for **Veritopinion**. The design seamlessly merges the original **Veritolytics** visual identity (the iconic 3D perspective Royal Blue badge with dark navy `V`, vivid color palette, and midnight navy **EST. 2017** heritage pill) with the new name **Veritopinion** and its distinctive interlocking **`op`** centerpiece.

---

## 1. Master Brand Logo (Primary)

The primary master logo is generated at ultra-high resolution with 4x supersampled anti-aliasing and a transparent background. It features the signature 3D perspective badge, the royal blue wordmark, the vivid red-orange and royal blue interlocking `op` symbol, and the midnight navy `EST. 2017` pill.

![Official Veritopinion Logo](../design-assets/branding/veritopinion-supersampled-b.png)
